# Preserved original study

The original confirmation grid has 40 units (four datasets, two models, five run seeds). Its unscaled adaptive score raises AURC in 23 of 30 primary controlled-shift comparisons, lowers it in five and ties in two. These outcomes remain the historical motivating baseline. Revised comparisons use common new checkpoints/cases and remain separate.

The original Electricity/XGB fitted models and probabilities are identical across seeds; its score variation comes from explanation/monitor randomization. The revised study uses 80% row/column sampling and common data/explanation/monitor randomization, producing five different fitted models in every group.

The two original returned ZIPs are preserved as release assets with their original names and checksums.
